# Pretrained recurrence V3: final-answer qualifier

Date: 2026-09-27. Status: separate post-audit design, before any V3 model output. V0, V1, and V2 remain terminal at their registered stops. The V2 answer audit is diagnostic, not a rescore or proof of recurrence benefit.

## Objective and single hypothesis

Test whether a bounded, deterministic final-answer grammar can yield an informative three-family benchmark on **fresh** Qwen3-1.7B F0 examples, after which the registered shared-block recurrence versus ordinary/compute-matched LoRA question can finally be evaluated. The audit found 60 mathematically correct final values among 93 V2-invalid development answers and 23 genuinely wrong values after a separate graph adjudication. Thus measurement repair has a concrete mechanism, but a passing V3 gate or training gain is not guaranteed. This is one new protocol, not iterative adjustment to V2.

Keep the pinned `Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B` snapshot/revision, BF16 eager/cacheless greedy inference, thinking disabled, shared block `[12,16)`, 256 generated-token and 512 total-token limits, list prompt/training view from V2, and expression/graph prompts unchanged. No few-shot examples, teacher-answer generation, prompt search, new family, or offloading work. Use the same candidate difficulties and candidate-1-first selection policy: expression leaves 6/7, list length 4/5 with operation count 1/2, graph vertices 7/8. All task data are generated deterministically from latent specifications with objective `interpret` answers.

## V3-only scorer, frozen before output

Create `answer_v3.py`; never edit `answer_v1.py` or `answer_v2.py`. The public scoring interface and score-record schema match V2. Require a nonempty complete EOS response. Extract from the response alone, without reading the gold answer or latent interpreter, then compare the unique parsed value with `interpret`. Use latent input only **after extraction** to check list length and graph query endpoints. A missing cue, incomplete/malformed final value, conflict between explicit final candidates, length mismatch, or non-EOS response is `invalid_format`; a unique well-formed but mathematically wrong value is `wrong_value`. Preserve evidence span and graph explanation status separately.

- Expressions: the exact case-insensitive, start-of-line cue phrases are `Final answer`, `Final result`, `Answer`, `So the final answer is`, and `So the final result is`. Do **not** accept bare `Result`, in-line mentions, quoted/fenced lines, or a phrase followed immediately by an extra word. Permit leading spaces/tabs, then optionally one Markdown heading marker of 1–6 `#` plus whitespace, and optionally one balanced `**...**` or `__...__` wrapper around the cue; a colon and spaces/tabs may follow the cue. Search at most 256 characters after it, stopping at the next cue. The answer is a signed integer that may be bare (`-12`), `\boxed{-12}`, `$-12$`, `$$-12$$`, `**-12**`, `$ \boxed{-12} $`, or `$$` newline `\boxed{-12}` newline `$$`. Allow up to two properly nested, balanced math/bold layers around one scalar, including intervening whitespace/newlines. A terminal period is allowed; other trailing prose or a second numeral within the cue payload makes that cue incomplete. `### Final Answer: $13$` and `So the final result is:\n$$\n\boxed{0}\n$$` are positive; `Final answer: **3** and then **4**`, `Result: 3`, and `> Final answer: 3` are negative. Distinct values after multiple cues or any later incomplete cue invalidate. Do not extract intermediate calculations.
- Lists: recognize `Answer`, `Final answer`, `Final result`, `Final list`, `So the final list is`, and the observed `So the final list after applying ADD1 once is` cue. Accept one complete bracketed integer vector within 256 characters after the cue and before any next final cue, with light Markdown/math/boxed wrapping; reject a later incomplete cue, malformed/partial vector, wrong vector length, and distinct vectors across final cues. This window admits the observed blank-line, `$$`, boxed-vector form. Do not treat the numeric list in the prompt or an intermediate step as a final answer. Parse an integer `10` as a value (usually wrong for ADD1 modulo 10), not as a format error.
- Graphs: retain the frozen V2 graph-answer extraction and separate V1 explanation assessment, including conflict rejection and endpoint matching. Bind all reused V1/V2 scorer files in the V3 run signature. Do not widen graph grammar based on V3 output.

The executable grammar is line-anchored: ignore cue-looking quoted lines (Markdown `>` and fenced code), match only the listed final phrases with optional Markdown heading/bold decoration, and examine no more than 256 response characters after each cue, stopping before the next cue. Strip only balanced `$`, `$$`, bold, and `\boxed{}` wrappers around a **single complete** scalar/vector; do not accept a prefix numeral, a partial list, arbitrary trailing value-bearing text, or an unbalanced wrapper. A cue with no complete candidate invalidates the response even if an earlier cue was valid. Equal complete candidates may agree; any distinct values invalidate. Tests must pin numeral boundaries (`3` versus `30`, signs, punctuation), quoted cues, equal/conflicting cues, and malformed later cues. Graph syntax extraction may reuse V2: collect response-only yes/no cues and stated path endpoints, validate stated endpoints against the latent start/end that generated the prompt (query parameters, not the gold reachability answer), then compare the unique result with `interpret`.

Build adversarial RED/GREEN tests from synthetic responses and the observed V2 failure *shapes*. Keep the 30 accepted blind-control reviews separate from scorer-development examples. **Freeze and commit the scorer/test hashes first**, then run a one-shot read-only check against those 30 previously reviewed controls; require agreement with the authenticated accepted final-answer labels for all 30 before V3 GPU output. The controls are a stratified compatibility check, not a representative fresh accuracy estimate. A discrepancy stops this V3 candidate before output for separate diagnosis; do not patch the scorer and silently repeat that check under the same V3 candidate. After V3 output no grammar edit can change the run.

## Gates and permitted continuation

One production `prepare`, then score **all six** fresh F0 pilots in fixed family/candidate order, even when c1 already qualifies. Candidate 1 wins if F0 accuracy is strictly between 5% and 95% and invalid rate <=10%; only otherwise may candidate 2 be selected. If neither qualifies, candidate 1 is the fixed fallback; c2's measured result never overrides an eligible c1. Fewer than two eligible families stops before development/sealed generation. If two qualify, freeze selected development and sealed splits and score development F0 only. Development requires three-family macro accuracy strictly between 5% and 95%, global invalid <=10%, and at least two individually informative families under the same 5%-95% and <=10% rule. The nonqualifying fallback remains in macro/global metrics. No candidate 3, threshold relaxation, output-length change, prompt rewrite, or post-output scorer patch.

Carry V2's 7,200 CUDA-second and 7,200 wall-second calibration caps, 86,400 CUDA-second study cap, 20 GiB artifact cap, and explicit incomplete/environment/resource statuses. If calibration fails, seal a terminal report; do not run frozen controls or LoRA. If it passes, implement/run F0/F1/F2 frozen recurrence controls and FineWeb retention, followed by paired A1 ordinary LoRA, A2 recurrent LoRA, and A3 training-time-matched ordinary LoRA with V2's hyperparameters, seed 47, paired metrics, two-point gain criterion, family-regression bound, and PPL point/bootstrap upper bound <=1.02. Only a passed discovery gate permits seed-48 confirmation and V3 sealed scoring. Added inference time/layer calls/VRAM remain reported. Success concerns post-training reasoning on this benchmark, **not** an MoE-like footprint or deployment advantage.

The implementation plan is calibration-first. Do not spend training GPU time or implement every later training stage before a valid fresh F0 gate. Push reviewed milestones on `exp`; do not merge `main` without user approval. Keep the unrelated Task 14 notes untouched.
