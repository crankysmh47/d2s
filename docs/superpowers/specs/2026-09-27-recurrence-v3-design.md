## V3-only scorer, frozen before output

Create `answer_v3.py`; never edit `answer_v1.py` or `answer_v2.py`. The public scoring interface and score-record schema match V2. Require a nonempty complete EOS response. Extract from the response alone, without reading the gold answer or latent interpreter, then compare the unique parsed value with `interpret`. Use latent input only **after extraction** to check list length and graph query endpoints. A missing cue, incomplete/malformed final value, conflict between explicit final candidates, length mismatch, or non-EOS response is `invalid_format`; a unique well-formed but mathematically wrong value is `wrong_value`. Preserve evidence span and graph explanation status separately.

- Expressions: the exact case-insensitive, start-of-line cue phrases are `Final answer`, `Final result`, `Answer`, `So the final answer is`, and `So the final result is`. Do **not** accept bare `Result`, in-line mentions, quoted/fenced lines, or a phrase followed immediately by an extra word. Permit leading spaces/tabs, then optionally one Markdown heading marker of 1–6 `#` plus whitespace, and optionally one balanced `**...**` or `__...__` wrapper around the cue; a colon and spaces/tabs may follow the cue. Search at most 256 characters after it, stopping at the next cue. The answer is a signed integer that may be bare (`-12`), `\boxed{-12}`, `$-12$`, `$$-12$$`, `**-12**`, `$ \boxed{-12} $`, or `$$` newline `\boxed{-12}` newline `$$`. Allow up to two properly nested, balanced math/bold layers around one scalar, including intervening whitespace/newlines. A terminal period is allowed; other trailing prose or a second numeral within the cue payload makes that cue incomplete. `### Final Answer: $13$` and `So the final result is:\n$$\n\boxed{0}\n$$` are positive; `Final answer: **3** and then **4**`, `Result: 3`, and `> Final answer: 3` are negative. Distinct values after multiple cues or any later incomplete cue invalidate. Do not extract intermediate calculations.
- Lists: recognize `Answer`, `Final answer`, `Final result`, `Final list`, `So the final list is`, and the observed `So the final list after applying ADD1 once is` cue. Accept one complete bracketed integer vector within 256 characters after the cue and before any next final cue, with light Markdown/math/boxed wrapping; reject a later incomplete cue, malformed/partial vector, wrong vector length, and distinct vectors across final cues. This window admits the observed blank-line, `$$`, boxed-vector form. Do not treat the numeric list in the prompt or an intermediate step as a final answer. Parse an integer `10` as a value (usually wrong for ADD1 modulo 10), not as a format error.
- Graphs: retain the frozen V2 graph-answer extraction and separate V1 explanation assessment, including conflict rejection and endpoint matching. Bind all reused V1/V2 scorer files in the V3 run signature. Do not widen graph grammar based on V3 output.

The executable grammar is line-anchored: ignore cue-looking quoted lines (Markdown `>` and fenced code), match only the listed final phrases with optional Markdown heading/bold decoration, and examine no more than 256 response characters after each cue, stopping before the next cue. Strip only balanced `$`, `$$`, bold, and `\boxed{}` wrappers around a **single complete** scalar/vector; do not accept a prefix numeral, a partial list, arbitrary trailing value-bearing text, or an unbalanced wrapper. A cue with no complete candidate invalidates the response even if an earlier cue was valid. Equal complete candidates may agree; any distinct values invalidate. Tests must pin numeral boundaries (`3` versus `30`, signs, punctuation), quoted cues, equal/conflicting cues, and malformed later cues. Graph syntax extraction may reuse V2: collect response-only yes/no cues and stated path endpoints, validate stated endpoints against the latent start/end that generated the prompt (query parameters, not the gold reachability answer), then compare the unique result with `interpret`.

Build adversarial RED/GREEN tests from synthetic responses and the observed V2 failure *shapes*. No scorer/test edit followed that access, but the original preregistered blinding condition was broken. Preserve that incident and do not count an agreement check on those 30 as V3 qualification.
